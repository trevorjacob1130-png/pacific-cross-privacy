Privacy Policy

Pacific Cross Messenger Assistant · Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Facebook Messenger assistant (“the Assistant”, “we”, “us”) is operated by an authorized Pacific Cross Philippines insurance agent. It answers inquiries sent to our Facebook Page and helps connect interested people with a human insurance agent. This policy explains what information we collect through the Assistant and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Messages you send to our Facebook Page through Messenger.
  • Contact details you choose to share in the conversation, such as your name, phone number, or email address, when you ask for a quote or a callback.
  • A Facebook-provided sender ID that lets us reply to you within Messenger. We do not receive your Facebook password or access your Facebook profile beyond what Messenger provides to reply.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions about Pacific Cross insurance plans and coverage.
  • To connect you with a human insurance agent for exact quotes, underwriting, and enrollment.
  • If you share contact details, to pass them to our agent so they can follow up with you.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Service providers who process your messages

To operate the Assistant, message content may be processed by the following providers solely to deliver this service:

  • Meta Platforms — provides the Messenger platform used to send and receive messages.
  • Anthropic — its Claude AI service generates the Assistant’s replies from the text of your messages.
  • Resend — used to email your contact details to our agent when you request follow-up.

Data retention

Conversation context is held only temporarily to maintain the flow of a chat and is not stored in a permanent database. When you provide contact details for follow-up, those details are emailed to our agent and retained by the agent for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your choices

  • You can stop messaging the Page at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete the contact information you shared by contacting us at the address below.

Children

The Assistant is intended for adults inquiring about insurance and is not directed to children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent change.
